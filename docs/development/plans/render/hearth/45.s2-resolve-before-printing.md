A step not in the plan (ruling 178), hearth 45's, after 45.2. render.md,
5.7: "`hearth render --resolve` and `hearth diff --resolve` … resolve each
needed key, one at a time (5.5), and record each closure before they
print"; 45.2's Proof: "each recorded before the diff prints". Render does
(`call/resolution.rs`, `resolved_answer`: every Resolve before the first
spec). The diff does not: at hearth 47d1170, `Comparison::answer`
(`call.rs:705`) calls `resolve_changed` (`call/comparison.rs:16`), which
looks only at `self.names.get(self.next)` (`:25`), so a second changed
target's keys are resolved after the first target's change has been put
out. The test `changed_keys_are_resolved_once_from_then_to_and_recorded_before_the_changes`
(`tests/diff/resolution.rs:73`) passes only because its crossed fixture's
first target holds both keys.

### 45.s2 Every changed target's keys before the first change
Scope: the order of a diff's Resolve requests against its changes; no
output's content changes.
Needs: 45.2.
Files: render `src/call.rs` (`Comparison`, `:557-569`, its literal
`:578-590`), `src/call/comparison.rs`, `src/tests/diff/resolution.rs`,
`src/tests/fixtures/diff_resolution.rs` (beside `crossed_resolution_diff`,
`:174`), `src/tests/fixtures/resolution.rs` (beside `KEY_B`, `:27`).
Shape: `Comparison` gains `resolving: usize`, a cursor over `names`,
beside `next`, `0` in `Call::diff`. `resolve_changed` takes the target at
`resolving`, not at `next`, and moves the cursor past each target that
asks no Resolve (unchanged key, a side without the target, or both keys
recorded or attempted), until it asks one or passes the last name; only
then does `answer` put out the change of `names[0]`. The side swaps
(`swap_resolution_side`, `inherit_finding`), the from-then-to order of a
target's keys, `next` and the emission of changes stay as they are.
Test shape: fixtures `KEY_C` and `KEY_D` (`Digest([3; 32])`, `[4; 32]`),
and `disjoint_resolution_diff()`: from x1-prod `KEY_A`, z1-prod `KEY_C`;
to x1-prod `KEY_B`, z1-prod `KEY_D`. Test
`every_changed_targets_keys_are_recorded_before_the_first_change`: the
outputs are Resolve and Record of `KEY_A`, `KEY_B`, `KEY_C`, `KEY_D`, in
that order, then the changes of x1-prod and z1-prod, both `Known`. On
47d1170 it fails at the third Resolve, with x1-prod's change in its place.
Proof: focused, `heavy cargo nextest run -p hearth-domain-render -E
'test(/resolution/)'`, the new test failing on main first and passing
after; then hearth's gate through `land`.
Don't: change render's order, a key's from-then-to order, the failed
key's error after the changes (`a_failed_resolve_keeps_new_key_and_its_error_follows_the_changes`),
or 28.4's world test.
Size: at most 90 lines.
