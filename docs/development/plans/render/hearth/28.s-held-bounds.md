# hearth 28.s: render holds at once what 8.4 lists, and the 1 GiB test

Steps not in the plan (ruling 192), hearth 28's, after 28.5, in one fresh
hearth session, in order: 28.s1 to 28.s3, 28.s8 (ruling 200), 28.s4, 28.s7 (ruling 198), 28.s5, 28.s6. The designer's answer to 28.5's question (hearth
36959af) amended render.md 2.3, section 3, 7.1 and 8.4, and asks for "a step
the supervisor names [that] builds the bounds below and lands
`the_shipped_limits_have_a_worst_case_within_1_gib`". At hearth c0184e6,
render's `limits::worst_case` (`limits.rs:58`) is about 2.3 × 10^14 bytes,
`entries::worst_case` (`entries.rs:655`) about 2.1 × 10^14, the `Collector`
(`errors.rs:204`) 272 MB, and the service's sum (`Client::worst_case`,
`hearth-service-builder/src/lib.rs:475`) adds `domain.max(protocol) ×
queue` (`:493`) to them.

Rules for every step here:
- **8.4's "Each layer's formula is its peak:** what the layer holds at once
  at its limits, not the sum of every maximum it reaches in turn" holds for
  every term of `Client::worst_case`, not render's alone (ruling 192).
- **A formula changes only with its memory case:** a `Counting` test in the
  layer's `tests/<layer>/tests/memory.rs` (`Memory::Checked`) drives the
  layer at the peak the formula names and holds the live heap within it at
  every iteration.
- **A bound's failure is 2.3's "What a limit fails":** the error names the
  path at fault, and fails what holds the excess.
- **No default changes,** and no limit is removed (the answer: not (c)).
- **Size:** each increment's first report gives its estimate; one past 600
  lines splits.

### 28.s1 The errors kept: 256, and 16 MiB of the bytes they carry
Scope: 7.1: "The errors kept are the longest start of that order that holds
at most 256 errors and 16 MiB of the bytes they carry, every string of every
field counted"; one sorting after the first dropped is counted, not kept.
Needs: 28.5.
Files: render `src/errors.rs` (`Collector`, `:133`; `push`, `:156` and
`:228`; `worst_case`, `:204`), `src/limits.rs` (a field for the 16 MiB, in
`Limits`, `:3`, and `SHIPPED`, `:27`), each `Limits` literal the compiler
names, render's `src/tests/errors.rs`, `tests/render/tests/memory.rs`.
Shape: the Collector counts each kept error's string bytes; the first error
that would pass 256 or 16 MiB is not kept, nor any after it in the order,
and the count goes on. `Collector::worst_case` is 256 errors' records and
16 MiB.
Test shape: unit `errors_past_16_mib_are_counted_not_kept` (errors whose
strings pass 16 MiB together: the kept are the longest start within it, the
count includes the rest); memory
`collecting_errors_at_the_limits_holds_no_more_than_the_worst_case`.
Don't: cut a string; change the order or 7.3's printing.

### 28.s2 What is decoded counts as it is held
Scope: 2.3: "What is decoded counts as it is held, not by its file's bytes:

lines, whose first bytes it keeps (8.3): every line, a comment's among

Files: render `src/revision.rs` (`Budget`, `:315`; `take`, `:325`),

Test shape: unit `a_document_of_list_elements_counts_32_bytes_each`:
